Slappybag's Toolbag

Free printable cards for tabletop RPGs. Every tool works the same way:

  1. Build a card, or import one.
  2. Save it to your library.
  3. Pick cards and print a sheet or save a PDF.

Paste anything

Copy a creature, hazard, item, spell or feat from Archives of Nethys (from the title line down) and paste it here. It opens in the right tool with the card filled in.
