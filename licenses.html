Pictures

The Find picture button searches three free sources and keeps only pictures you may print and sell:

  • game-icons.net: icons by Lorc, Delapouite and other contributors, CC BY 3.0 (a few CC0).
  • Wikimedia Commons: public-domain paintings and engravings, plus CC BY and CC0 pictures.
  • Openverse: CC0, public-domain-mark and CC BY pictures.

Every picture keeps its credit, and printed sheets list the credits of the pictures on each page. Pictures you add yourself are your responsibility.
